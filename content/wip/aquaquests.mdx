# AquaQuests

Daily, weekly and story quests with a shared progression track, designed to plug into whatever
economy and rank plugins you already run.

<Callout type="warning">
  This plugin is still in development. No information here should be treated as final.
</Callout>

## Planned scope

- Quest types: kill, mine, craft, fish, travel, custom-event.
- Rotating daily and weekly pools with per-player seeds.
- Multi-step story chains with branching rewards.
- Progression track with milestone rewards.
- Placeholders for every counter, so any scoreboard can show progress.
